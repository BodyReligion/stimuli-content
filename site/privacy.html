1. Who we are

STiMUL-i is provided by Body Religion Limited (“Body Religion”, “we”, “us”), a company registered in England & Wales under number 9977560, whose registered office is Office 140, 30 Red Lion Street, Richmond, London, TW9 1RB, United Kingdom. We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

If you have any questions about this policy or your information, contact us at info@stimul-i.com.

2. The short version

  • You don’t need an account to use STiMUL-i, and we don’t ask for your name or email address in the App.
  • Your sessions, library, imported files, recordings and settings are stored on your device. We don’t upload them to our servers.
  • The App has no advertising and no advertising trackers, and we don’t sell your information.
  • Apple or Google take payment for subscriptions. We never see your card details.
  • Some features connect to other services, such as YouTube, AI providers you choose, and the server that delivers Store downloads. This policy explains what they receive.

3. Information that stays on your device

The following is created and kept on your device by the App. We do not collect it, and it is removed when you delete the App (unless you have saved copies elsewhere):

  • your sessions, layers, triggers, presets and settings;
  • images, videos, audio and statements you import or create, and Store Content you have downloaded;
  • video and audio recordings you make with the App;
  • heart rate readings from a connected Bluetooth wearable, which are used only to sync your session in real time and are not stored or sent anywhere;
  • any AI API keys you add, and any affiliate or offer code you enter.

Your device’s own backup service (such as iCloud or Google backup) may include App data in your device backups, under your agreement with Apple or Google.

4. Device permissions

The App asks for permission before it uses these device features, and you can change your choice at any time in your device settings:

  • Camera: to use your camera as a layer and to record sessions.
  • Microphone: to record your voice for soundtracks and to sync layers to sound.
  • Photos and files: to import your own images, videos and audio, and to save your recordings.
  • Bluetooth: to connect a heart rate monitor or other wearable.
  • Notifications: to show the daily reminder you can switch on. These reminders are scheduled on your device; we don’t send push notifications from a server.

Camera and microphone input is processed on your device and is only saved if you choose to record.

5. Information we and our providers receive

Subscriptions and purchases

When you subscribe, Apple or Google process your payment and share with us transaction information such as the product bought, price, currency, country, the date of purchase and renewal, and any offer code used. They do not share your name, email address or payment details with us. We use a subscription management service, RevenueCat Inc., to check whether your subscription is active. It receives an anonymous ID created by the App, your purchase receipts and the offer code used. We use this to give you access, to count sales made with each affiliate code, and to keep our financial records.

Store downloads and the home screen video

Store Content, the Store catalogue and the home screen video are delivered from GitHub Pages, a service run by GitHub, Inc. Like any web server, it receives your device’s IP address and basic technical information (such as browser or app version) when you download something, and may keep this in its logs for security purposes. We don’t receive a record of which items you download.

YouTube

The App and the Website show our YouTube channel. Loading the channel list or playing a video connects your device to YouTube, which receives your IP address and device information and may set cookies or use similar technology. Google’s Privacy Policy applies.

AI features you choose to use

If you add your own API key for OpenAI, Anthropic or Google Gemini, the App sends your prompt, and where relevant your image or settings, directly from your device to that provider so it can generate words, images or video. That provider handles the information under its own privacy policy and your agreement with it. We don’t receive your key, prompts or results.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, name and whatever you include in your message. We use this to reply and to help you.

Affiliates

If you join our affiliate programme, we collect your name, contact details, the code we issue you, the number of subscriptions made with your code, and the details we need to pay you and to meet our tax obligations.

6. Our website

The Website does not use analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels, and we don’t set any cookies. To display the page, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and, for the documentary video, a video player script from jsDelivr. Those services receive your IP address and browser information when they deliver these files. If you play a YouTube video, the YouTube section above applies.

7. Why we use information and our legal basis

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), we must have a legal basis for using personal information:

  • To provide the Products and your subscription, including checking access and applying offer codes: necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • To pay affiliates and keep financial records: necessary for our contract with the affiliate and to meet our legal obligations, such as tax and accounting rules.
  • To reply to your messages, keep the Products secure and fix problems: our legitimate interests in running and improving our business, which we balance against your rights.
  • To send you marketing emails, if you ask us to: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.
  • To comply with the law or respond to lawful requests from authorities: our legal obligations.

8. Who we share information with

We only share personal information with:

  • service providers who help us run the Products, such as Apple, Google, RevenueCat, GitHub and our email provider, who may only use it to provide their services to us;
  • our professional advisers, such as accountants and lawyers;
  • authorities, when the law requires it or to protect the rights, property or safety of Body Religion, our users or others;
  • a buyer or successor if we sell or reorganise all or part of our business, in which case they may use it only as described in this policy.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for advertising.

9. International transfers

Some of our providers, including Apple, Google, RevenueCat and GitHub, are based in or process information in the United States and other countries outside the UK. Where personal information is transferred outside the UK, we make sure it is protected, for example by relying on the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on the International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum approved under UK law.

10. How long we keep information

  • Information on your device stays there until you delete it or delete the App.
  • Subscription and transaction records are kept for as long as you subscribe and then for up to 7 years, as UK tax and accounting rules require.
  • Emails are kept for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry, and then for up to 2 years unless there is an ongoing issue.
  • Affiliate records are kept for as long as you are an affiliate and then for up to 7 years for tax purposes.

We may keep anonymised information, which cannot identify you, for statistics.

11. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect the personal information we hold, including encrypted connections and restricted access. Because most of your STiMUL-i data stays on your device, please keep your device protected with a passcode and up to date. No method of transmitting information over the internet is completely secure, so we can’t guarantee the security of information sent to us.

12. Children

STiMUL-i is intended for adults. Subscriptions are for people aged 18 or over, and anyone younger should use the App only with a parent or guardian’s involvement and consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use your information;
  • ask for your information in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

To make a request, email info@stimul-i.com. We may need to confirm your identity first. We will respond within one month and there is normally no charge. Some rights have limits; for example, we must keep certain financial records by law. Because we don’t hold your App content, the quickest way to delete it is to delete it in the App or delete the App.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK’s data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. If you live in the European Economic Area or Switzerland, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Products or the law change. We will post the new version on this page with a new “last updated” date and, if the changes are significant, tell you in the App before they take effect.
