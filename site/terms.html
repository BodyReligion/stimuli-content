9. Health and safety

Photosensitive epilepsy warning. STiMUL-i shows flashing lights, flickering images and rapidly changing patterns. In a small number of people these can trigger seizures, even where there is no history of epilepsy. Do not use STiMUL-i if you have epilepsy, a history of seizures or photosensitivity, or if anyone in your family does, without first getting your doctor’s advice. Stop using the App immediately and seek medical advice if you notice dizziness, altered vision, eye or face twitching, jerking or shaking of the arms or legs, disorientation, confusion or loss of awareness.

  • Not a medical device. STiMUL-i is a wellness and creative tool. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, monitor or prevent any disease or medical condition. Nothing in the Products is medical advice. References to research are for general information only and do not mean that the Products have been shown to produce any particular result.
  • Talk to a professional first if you are pregnant, have a heart condition or a pacemaker, a mental health condition (including anxiety, depression, psychosis or a history of trauma), a neurological condition, or any other health concern. Intense audiovisual stimulation, binaural beats, EMDR-style eye movement and suggestion-based content can bring up strong emotions. EMDR features in the App are not EMDR therapy and are not a substitute for a qualified therapist.
  • Stay safe while using it. Never use STiMUL-i while driving, cycling, operating machinery or doing anything that needs your full attention. Use it in a well-lit room, sit at a comfortable distance from the screen, keep the volume at a safe level and take regular breaks. Use Safe mode to keep flashing at a gentler rate.
  • Heart rate. Heart rate readings from a connected wearable are used only to sync your session. They are not medical measurements and must not be relied on for health decisions.
  • You use the Products at your own risk and are responsible for deciding whether they are suitable for you.
